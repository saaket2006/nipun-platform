FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know About Nipun

Clear answers regarding semantic screening, explainability, role scoring weights, and applicant data security.

How does Semantic Matching differ from legacy keyword ATS filters?
Keyword matchers require exact textual substring matches, rejecting qualified applicants who use synonyms (e.g., K8s vs. Kubernetes or JS vs. ECMAScript). Nipun parses candidate resumes into conceptual semantic vectors, understanding context, aliases, and seniority without rigid lexical brittleness.
How does Explainable AI (XAI) justify candidate match scores?
Instead of generating opaque, untraceable scores, Nipun breaks down every assessment into explicit component contributions: Skills, Experience Tenure, Education, and Projects. Each score includes natural-language justifications showing exactly which requirements were met or missed.
Can hiring teams customize scoring coefficients for specialized engineering roles?
Yes. Recruiters can configure dynamic Scoring Profiles (e.g., Senior Backend Engineer, Cloud DevOps, AI/ML Specialist, or Early Career). Each profile lets teams tune the relative weights of skills, years of verified experience, educational degrees, and production projects.
How can candidates use Nipun to evaluate and improve their resumes?
Candidates can upload their resumes against specific job descriptions to receive an objective, transparent fit assessment. Nipun highlights missing technical skills, tenure alignment, and version history comparisons to help applicants refine their profiles with confidence.
How is applicant privacy, security, and data protection handled?
All applicant records and documents are protected with role-based access controls (RBAC) and Firebase Authentication. Candidates maintain full privacy over their personal profile updates, and evaluation data is never shared across unauthorized tenant workspaces.
